Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to Play Friends ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you download, install, and use the Play Friends mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect minimal data necessary for user authentication, account security, and gameplay:

  • Email Address: Used for account creation, one-time passcode (OTP) verification via Brevo, and password resets.
  • Phone Number (Optional): Used as an alternative account identifier and player contact security.
  • Player Profile & In-Game Data: Username, avatar choice, game trophies, and win/loss statistics.

2. Strictly Non-Gambling & Free-to-Play

Play Friends is strictly a casual, entertainment application. We do NOT provide:

  • Real-money betting or wagering.
  • Cash prizes or payout conversions.
  • Paid loot boxes or real-money in-game gambling.

All in-game coins, level points, and trophies are purely virtual demo points with zero cash value.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Play Friends mobile application.
  • To send transactional 6-digit OTP verification emails for authentication.
  • To display player rankings, matches played, and local gameplay stats.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only utilize trusted third-party infrastructure for transactional services:

  • Brevo (formerly Sendinblue): Transactional email service used securely over encrypted HTTPS connections to deliver one-time OTP verification codes.

5. Data Security & Encryption

All network traffic and API communications are transmitted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS) encryption to safeguard your data against unauthorized access.

6. User Data Deletion & Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated profile data at any time. To request data deletion, contact us at playgamefriendes@gmail.com with your registered email.

7. Children's Privacy

Play Friends is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
